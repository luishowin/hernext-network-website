Why we exist

We believe the future will not be shaped by potential alone. It will be shaped by the opportunities we create together.

Across the continent, women establish businesses, strengthen industries, drive agricultural productivity, advance research and lead organisations. Their leadership and innovation represent one of Africa's greatest assets. Yet many continue to encounter barriers that limit access to finance, markets, leadership, technology and strategic networks.

Signature initiatives

Turning vision into action

Six programmes translate our purpose into practical opportunity, strengthening partnerships and contributing to Africa's long-term development.

01

HerNext Leadership Academy

A leadership development platform equipping women with the knowledge, confidence and skills required to lead organisations, influence policy and drive sustainable change.

02

HerNext Opportunity Hub

A collaborative platform connecting women with mentorship, business support, professional networks, funding opportunities, markets and strategic partnerships.

03

Global Trade and Investment Platform

An initiative promoting regional and international trade by connecting women entrepreneurs with buyers, investors, trade missions, exhibitions and global business opportunities.

04

Innovation and Sustainability Forum

A platform encouraging innovation, technology adoption and sustainable business practice, while promoting dialogue on Africa's future economy.

05

HerNext Partnership Forum

An annual platform bringing governments, corporations, development partners, financial institutions, academia and entrepreneurs together to explore collaborative solutions.

06

Research and Policy Dialogue

A thought leadership initiative promoting research, policy discussion and evidence-based recommendations on women's economic transformation.
