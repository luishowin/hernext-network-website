No institution creates lasting impact alone. HerNext builds partnerships that bring together governments, businesses, development organisations, financial institutions, academia and civil society to achieve shared objectives.

Expanding access to capital, investment and financial inclusion.

🌐

Development Partners

🎓

Academic Institutions

Generating knowledge, research and evidence that informs decision-making.
