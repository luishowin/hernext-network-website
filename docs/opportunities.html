Opportunities & Programmes

Turning Vision Into Action

HerNext's signature initiatives translate our vision into practical programmes that create opportunities and contribute to Africa's long-term development.

Strategic Pillars

Our work is guided by eight strategic pillars that reflect our commitment to creating sustainable opportunities for women across Africa.

  • 01
    Leadership Development

    Investing in ethical, visionary and capable leaders who inspire positive change.

  • 02
    Entrepreneurship & Enterprise Development

    Supporting business growth, enterprise resilience and sustainable success.

  • 03
    Opportunity Creation

    Expanding access to knowledge, markets, mentorship, finance and partnerships.

  • 04
    Trade & Market Access

    Promoting domestic, regional and international trade linkages.

  • 05
    Investment Promotion

    Strengthening Africa's investment ecosystem through collaboration.

  • 06
    Innovation & Technology

    Encouraging digital transformation and research that improves competitiveness.

  • 07
    Capacity Development

    Strengthening skills through training, mentorship and professional development.

  • 08
    Research & Thought Leadership

    Promoting evidence-informed dialogue on Africa's development.

Signature Initiatives

HerNext Leadership Academy

A leadership development platform designed to equip women with the knowledge, confidence and skills required to lead organisations, influence policy and drive sustainable change across Africa.

HerNext Opportunity Hub

A collaborative platform connecting women with mentorship, business support, professional networks, funding opportunities, markets and strategic partnerships that accelerate growth.

HerNext Global Trade & Investment Platform

An initiative that promotes regional and international trade by connecting women entrepreneurs with buyers, investors, trade missions, exhibitions and global business opportunities.

HerNext Innovation & Sustainability Forum

A platform that encourages innovation, technology adoption and sustainable business practices while promoting dialogue on Africa's future economy.

HerNext Partnership Forum

An annual platform that brings together governments, corporations, development partners, financial institutions, academia and entrepreneurs to explore collaborative solutions.

HerNext Research & Policy Dialogue

A thought leadership initiative that promotes research, policy discussions and evidence-based recommendations on women's economic transformation.
