Our impact

Creating meaningful and lasting change

Impact is measured not only by the activities we undertake, but by the lasting value created through our work. Our goal is to contribute to systems that continue creating opportunity long after individual initiatives have concluded.

Understanding the opportunity gap

Unlocking Africa's greatest resource

Across every sector, women are building businesses, creating employment, leading organisations and developing innovative solutions. Yet many continue to encounter barriers that prevent them from fully participating in economic development.

01

Access to finance

Limited access to affordable financing and investment capital continues to restrict business growth and innovation.

02

Market access

Many enterprises struggle to connect with domestic, regional and international markets where they can expand sustainably.

03

Leadership opportunities

Women remain underrepresented in many leadership and decision-making positions across both public and private sectors.

04

Technology and innovation

Unequal access to digital technologies, innovation ecosystems and technical skills can slow enterprise growth and competitiveness.

05

Business development support

Many entrepreneurs require mentorship, professional development, advisory services and institutional support to scale successfully.

06

Strategic networks

Meaningful partnerships and professional networks often determine access to opportunities, investment and markets.

Our position

These challenges represent opportunities for collaboration. Rather than viewing them as limitations, we believe they can be addressed through strategic partnerships, knowledge sharing, institutional collaboration and long-term investment in people and enterprises. Our work focuses on strengthening the systems that create opportunity rather than simply responding to individual challenges.

Our approach to impact

Five principles that shape how we work

01

Creating opportunities

Every initiative should expand access to opportunities that enable women to lead, innovate, build enterprises and contribute to economic transformation.

02

Strengthening institutions

Strong institutions create sustainable development. We support collaboration that strengthens organisations, businesses and ecosystems capable of delivering long-term impact.

03

Building partnerships

Complex challenges require collective action. We bring together governments, development partners, businesses, financial institutions, academia and civil society to pursue shared solutions.

04

Promoting innovation

Innovation enables societies to respond to changing economic, social and environmental realities. We encourage creativity, research and technology-driven solutions that improve competitiveness and resilience.

05

Advancing sustainable development

Economic growth must create value that endures. Our work promotes inclusive, responsible and sustainable approaches that contribute to future generations.

Measuring success

Outcomes, not activities

HerNext evaluates its contribution through the outcomes it helps create:

  • Expanded access to opportunities for women
  • Stronger women-led enterprises
  • Increased participation in leadership
  • Greater collaboration across sectors
  • Enhanced market access and trade opportunities
  • Sustainable strategic partnerships
  • Improved institutional capacity
  • Contributions to inclusive economic transformation

Sustainable Development Goals

Contributing to global and African development priorities

Our programmes and partnerships align closely with five of the Sustainable Development Goals, while remaining committed to solutions that reflect Africa's own realities, priorities and ambitions.

SDG 5, Gender Equality

Supporting women's leadership, entrepreneurship and economic participation.

SDG 8, Decent Work and Economic Growth

Promoting enterprise development, innovation and inclusive economic growth.

SDG 9, Industry, Innovation and Infrastructure

Encouraging industrial development, value addition and technological innovation.

SDG 13, Climate Action

Promoting environmentally responsible business practices and sustainable development.

SDG 17, Partnerships for the Goals

Strengthening collaboration between governments, development partners, businesses, academia and civil society.

Lasting impact is created when institutions strengthen systems rather than simply deliver activities.
