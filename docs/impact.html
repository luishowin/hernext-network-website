Our impact

Creating meaningful and lasting change

At HerNext, impact is more than participation, activities or visibility. We measure success by the meaningful economic changes created in the lives, enterprises and livelihoods of the women we serve.

What impact means to us

From participation to progress

Our work begins with evidence. We listen to women, identify the barriers affecting their economic progress, design practical interventions with the right partners and measure whether those interventions create measurable and sustainable change.

Our ambition is not simply to reach more women. It is to improve economic outcomes.

Numbers matter, but numbers alone do not tell the whole story. The number of women attending a programme tells us about reach. What happens afterwards tells us about impact. These are the six changes we look for.

01

Increased income and profitability

Whether women are earning more and building more financially sustainable enterprises.

02

Improved productivity

Whether farmers, producers and entrepreneurs are able to produce more efficiently, improve output or reduce unnecessary production costs.

03

Better market access

Whether women gain reliable buyers, enter new markets, secure contracts or improve the value they receive for their products and services.

04

Access to finance

Whether women become better positioned to access appropriate financing, investment or other productive capital.

05

Stronger enterprises

Whether participating businesses improve their operations, resilience, financial management and capacity for sustainable growth.

06

Sustainable livelihoods

Whether interventions contribute to stronger and more resilient sources of income for women and their households.

Impact in action

Starting with evidence

HerNext Kiambu Chapter. HerNext's community-level approach begins by listening. Following the establishment of the chapter, women from different economic activities were engaged to understand their enterprises, livelihoods and most significant barriers to growth.

Individual follow-up and baseline data collection were subsequently undertaken with women engaged in livestock enterprises, including dairy, poultry and pig farming. The assessment identified animal feed affordability and access as a major recurring challenge affecting participating livestock farmers.

Rather than immediately prescribing a solution, HerNext is using these findings to inform the design of a targeted intervention and identify appropriate technical, commercial and development partners. The intervention will be designed to assess changes in production costs, productivity, profitability, market readiness and household livelihoods.

This is the HerNext approach in practice: listen first, understand the evidence, intervene strategically and measure what changes.

From data to action

What happens next

Once a challenge has been identified, HerNext seeks partners capable of addressing different parts of the problem.

In livestock, for example, collaboration may include animal nutrition and feed companies, veterinary and technical experts, financial institutions, agricultural research organisations, government and county institutions, development partners, processors and buyers.

HerNext's role is to organise, connect, coordinate and measure, building an ecosystem around the economic challenge rather than attempting to solve every problem alone.

How we measure impact

Accountability through evidence

Each HerNext programme establishes relevant baseline indicators before implementation and measures progress over time.

Enterprise

Revenue growth, profitability, business survival, jobs created or sustained.

Agriculture

Production levels, input and feed costs, yield and productivity, mortality rates, farmer income.

Markets

Number of reliable buyers, sales volumes, value of contracts, new markets accessed.

Finance

Women accessing finance, value of finance accessed, investment readiness.

Trade

Businesses entering new markets, buyer connections, export opportunities converted into actual business.

We are committed to reporting both what works and what we learn, using evidence to strengthen future interventions. No figure appears on this page until follow-up measurement supports it.

Our commitment

Impact before visibility. Evidence before intervention. Sustainability before scale.

We believe visibility matters when it brings attention, resources and partnerships to meaningful work. But our ultimate measure of success is not how visible HerNext becomes. It is whether the women we work alongside are building stronger enterprises, accessing better markets, increasing their incomes and creating more sustainable livelihoods. That is the impact we seek to build.
