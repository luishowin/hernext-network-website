About HerNext

A platform for collaboration

HerNext Network is a Pan-African institution committed to creating pathways of opportunity that advance women's economic transformation through leadership, entrepreneurship, innovation, strategic partnerships and sustainable development.

Our story

Every institution begins with a purpose

HerNext began with a simple but important belief: Africa's future will be stronger when more women have access to opportunities that enable them to succeed.

Across the continent, women establish businesses, strengthen value chains, drive agricultural productivity, lead organisations, innovate across industries and create employment. Yet too often their progress is constrained by barriers that limit access to finance, markets, technology, leadership and strategic partnerships.

Recognising both the challenge and the immense opportunity, HerNext Network was established to help strengthen the ecosystems that enable women to realise their potential.

Our purpose is not simply to organise events or build networks. It is to create pathways through which women can access knowledge, partnerships, markets, investment, leadership opportunities and innovation ecosystems that support long-term success.

Our story is only beginning. Each partnership we build, each leader we inspire and each opportunity we help create becomes another chapter in the story of an institution dedicated to Africa's future.

Our foundation

The direction that shapes every decision

Our vision, mission, purpose and philosophy provide the direction that shapes our decisions, partnerships and long-term aspirations.

Vision

An Africa where women have equitable access to opportunities that enable them to lead, innovate, build sustainable enterprises and contribute meaningfully to inclusive economic transformation.

Mission

To create pathways of opportunity through leadership, entrepreneurship, innovation, strategic partnerships and sustainable development.

Purpose

To strengthen ecosystems that expand access to opportunity and contribute to Africa's long-term prosperity.

Our belief

Opportunity has the power to transform lives, strengthen businesses, build resilient communities and accelerate economic development. When women are provided with equitable access, they become catalysts for innovation, enterprise, job creation and sustainable growth.

Our philosophy

Potential is universal. Opportunity should be too. Every individual deserves access to opportunities that allow talent, innovation and ambition to flourish, and HerNext exists to help create those pathways.

Our promise

To build an institution characterised by integrity, collaboration, excellence and long-term impact, continuing to strengthen partnerships and expand opportunity across the continent.

The Africa we envision

A future built on opportunity

Africa is a continent of immense promise. It is home to the world's youngest population, rapidly growing economies, expanding regional markets, abundant natural resources, vibrant entrepreneurial ecosystems and a new generation of innovators and changemakers.

We believe Africa's future will not be defined by its challenges, but by how effectively it unlocks the potential of its people. We envision an Africa where opportunity is not determined by circumstance but by potential, ambition and innovation.

Women leading

Thriving enterprises, industrialisation, scientific discovery, stronger agricultural value chains, public policy and technological innovation across every sector of the economy.

Institutions collaborating

Governments, the private sector, academia, development partners and civil society working together to create an environment where enterprise and innovation flourish.

Prosperity that endures

Inclusive economic transformation that creates value for present and future generations, in businesses that compete globally and communities that are economically resilient.

The future belongs to societies that invest in people, strengthen institutions and create opportunities that allow innovation and enterprise to flourish.
