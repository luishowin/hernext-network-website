Who We Are

HerNext Network is a Pan-African institution committed to creating pathways of opportunity that advance women's economic transformation through leadership, entrepreneurship, innovation, strategic partnerships and sustainable development.

We bring together women, governments, development partners, private sector organisations, financial institutions, academia and civil society to strengthen ecosystems that expand opportunity and create sustainable impact.

Our Story

HerNext began with a simple but important belief: Africa's future will be stronger when more women have access to opportunities that enable them to succeed.

Across the continent, women continue to demonstrate extraordinary resilience, innovation and leadership. They establish businesses, strengthen value chains, drive agricultural productivity, lead organisations, and innovate across industries.

Yet too often, their progress is constrained by barriers that limit access to finance, markets, technology, leadership opportunities and strategic partnerships. Recognising both the challenge and the immense opportunity, HerNext Network was established to help strengthen the ecosystems that enable women to realise their potential.

Our story is only beginning. Each partnership we build, each leader we inspire and each opportunity we help create becomes another chapter in the story of an institution dedicated to Africa's future.
