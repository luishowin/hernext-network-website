Partners and collaboration

Strong partnerships create lasting impact

Collaboration is at the heart of HerNext Network. We believe meaningful and sustainable progress is achieved when institutions work together towards a common purpose.

Our philosophy

Each partner brings unique strengths

Together, partners create opportunities that no single organisation could achieve alone. For this reason, HerNext is committed to building partnerships founded on trust, transparency, shared values and mutual accountability.

We seek relationships that create long-term value rather than short-term outcomes. Our approach is collaborative rather than competitive, recognising that Africa's greatest opportunities will be realised through collective action.

By connecting people, institutions and ideas, we contribute to stronger ecosystems that enable women and their enterprises to thrive.

Who we partner with

Seven kinds of partner, one shared purpose

Governments

Create the enabling policies within which enterprise, trade and innovation can grow.

Private sector

Drives innovation and investment, opens supply chains and creates routes to market.

Financial institutions

Expand access to capital and build the investment readiness that enterprise growth depends on.

Development partners

Provide technical expertise and global experience that strengthen programme design and delivery.

Academic institutions

Generate the knowledge and research that inform policy and evidence-based decisions.

Civil society

Strengthens community engagement and keeps programmes grounded in real need.

Entrepreneurs

Create the solutions that improve lives, and carry the opportunity forward into their own communities.
