Why partner with HerNext

No single institution can do it alone

Economic challenges are interconnected. Skills need pathways to opportunity. Enterprises need markets and appropriate finance. Farmers need knowledge, reliable inputs and stronger market connections. Communities benefit when institutions work together around shared priorities.

HerNext helps connect these parts of the ecosystem. We work to identify needs, shape practical interventions, mobilise complementary capabilities and learn from implementation. We welcome partners who share our commitment to inclusive economic transformation, responsible collaboration and measurable progress.

How a partnership begins

From shared interest to practical action

  1. Shared need
  2. Understand
  3. Design
  4. Agree
  5. Implement
  6. Measure and learn
  1. Identify a shared need or opportunity.
  2. Listen to the people and organisations affected and understand the context.
  3. Agree on the intended outcomes and design a practical response.
  4. Confirm partner roles, resources, responsibilities and safeguards.
  5. Implement the agreed activities and communicate openly.
  6. Measure progress, learn and agree on next steps.

We seek partners around real needs, not partnerships simply for visibility.

Our partnership principles

Principles that guide every partnership

01

Shared purpose

We seek alignment around clearly understood needs and intended outcomes.

02

Mutual respect

We value the knowledge, experience and contribution of every partner and community.

03

Transparency

We aim to communicate openly about objectives, roles, resources, risks and progress.

04

Accountability

We take commitments seriously and encourage clear responsibilities and responsible reporting.

05

Inclusion

We seek to expand meaningful participation and ensure the people affected remain part of the conversation.

06

Sustainability

We value partnerships that build capability and support lasting outcomes.

07

Evidence and learning

We use experience and available evidence to improve how work is designed and delivered.
